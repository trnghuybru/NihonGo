## Final prompt

Use case: identity-preserve / stylized-concept. Create ONE production-ready TRANSPARENT PNG game character sprite atlas for NihonGO's 'Công thành Momo'. The reference is Momo's identity, NOT the outfit to keep. Preserve the same recognizable cute orange tanuki mascot: huge round warm brown eyes, dark brown eye mask, rounded ears, cream muzzle and belly, fluffy striped brown/orange tail, chubby chibi proportions. Redesign as polished 2D mobile game samurai characters, bold clean outlines and simple cel shading readable at 48–96 pixels, friendly determined expression. Brand palette peach orange #FCA04B, warm cream #FFF8F1, dark brown #252526; small muted gold trim only, no blue or neon. EXACTLY four separate full-body characters in a strict equal 2x2 grid on a square transparent canvas, each character centered completely inside its own quadrant with generous 12% transparent padding around the silhouette, all feet aligned at the same relative height in each tile, no artwork across tile boundaries. Same scale/proportions, three-quarter view facing RIGHT, standing in a ready-to-march game pose. Top-left tier 0 recruit: simple cream headband, small orange cloth vest and brown boots, no waving pose. Top-right tier 1 warrior: light peach lamellar armor, small sheathed sword, cream scarf, headband. Bottom-left tier 2 elite: more layered orange samurai armor, small open helmet that preserves both round ears and the entire face, short flowing cream cape, sword held low safely. Bottom-right tier 3 shogun: ornate but simple orange/cream armor with crescent helmet crest, short cream cape, gold trim, confident ready stance, no huge weapon or effects obscuring body. All four are clearly the SAME Momo mascot, animal paws and fluffy tails, NOT human bodies or human faces. Asset only, no environment, no ground, no floor shadow, no labels, no text, no UI, no logo, no grid lines, no watermark. Genuine alpha transparency, not a checkerboard painted into the picture. Keep every ear, tail, boot and sword fully within its quadrant. This atlas will be cropped at runtime into four game sprites.
